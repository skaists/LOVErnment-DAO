## What it is

**ln-vending** (GPL-3.0) — Node.js integration of the **MDB protocol** (the standard
vending-machine peripheral bus): Lightning payment → physical dispense. A working
payment-to-physical-goods loop on commodity hardware.

sight. When the festival-marketplace lane wants its hardware leg, this is the reference
